How it works

Three minutes from first message to a working secretary

Open the chat

Tap Start in Telegram. Eleanor asks your language, your name and your city, and offers a time for the morning brief. No forms.

Talk like you would to a person

Text or voice, no commands to learn. “What's on this week”, “log 25 for lunch”, “remember that Maria has the spare keys”.

She keeps things moving

Reminds, recalculates, spots overlapping meetings and broken promises. Anything she sets up has an Undo button underneath.

Under the hood

Serious engineering behind a friendly chat

Eleanor is not a chatbot with a to-do list bolted on. She is a system that reasons, remembers and acts, built to be trusted with real work.

  • Reasoning by Claude (Anthropic), the model behind the most careful AI assistants. Every answer can call her skills, in the right order, and explain what it did.
  • Voice and vector memory by Google Gemini. Speech in, speech out, and a multilingual search that finds “cameras for the shop” when you wrote about it in another language.
  • Three layers of memory: the live conversation, nightly facts and summaries, and a verbatim archive of everything you ever told her or sent.
  • Proactive, with manners. Twenty-eight background routines, from reminders to backups, each watched by a guard that reports when something breaks.
  • EU-hosted data, undoable actions, no training on your data.

Your data

Your secretary, not somebody's dataset

Everything Eleanor keeps can be taken away in one file or erased without a trace. The database and files are encrypted at rest and hosted in the EU. AI providers process your requests under API terms that forbid training on them.

  • /mydata exports everything as one file
  • /delete_me erases everything, including files and connected services
  • /facts shows what she remembers, /forget removes a single fact
Does Eleanor read my other chats?

No. Only your chat with her. Connected services are read only when you ask for something that needs them.
